English

Guandan is primarily a local desktop game. We do not actively collect personally identifiable information, and we do not use advertising SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs.

Epic Login & Entitlement Verification

If Epic login or entitlement verification is enabled, it is used only for local authorization and entitlement checks. It is not used for social posting, advertising, or third-party marketing.

Local Data Storage

The application may store necessary data locally, including settings, saves, replays, license files, and authorization-related cache information. These data are typically stored on the user's own device.

Future Changes

If future versions introduce online features, account features, or cloud sync, we will update this privacy policy accordingly.
