English

Introduction

Guandan is a desktop card game focused on the Chinese trick-taking game "Guandan". It supports offline play, local rule guidance, and is suitable for casual sessions and rule practice.

Features

  • Guandan rules and leveling rhythm
  • Tribute, counter-tribute, and "jie feng" guidance
  • Chinese and English UI
  • Offline voice narration
  • Local saves and replays
  • Playable offline

Note

The game primarily runs locally and does not require continuous online connectivity. If Epic login or entitlement verification is enabled, it is used only for local authorization checks and not for social posting or advertising purposes.
